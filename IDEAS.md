## 🧭 How we pick

Score each shortlisted idea 1–5 on:

- **Excitement**: will I still want to work on it in week 3?
- **Usefulness**: will I (or someone) actually use it?
- **Learning**: does it teach me something new?
- **Feasibility**: can I get a first version working in about 2 weekends?
